⚡ Connect Intervals.icu (Garmin)

Intervals.icu is 100% free and connects directly to Garmin Connect. When you finish a workout on your Garmin watch, it syncs automatically!

3 Easy Steps:
  1. Go to intervals.icu/settings.
  2. Make sure Garmin Connect is toggled ON (it auto-syncs your watch).
  3. Scroll down to Developer Settings on that page and copy your API Key.
